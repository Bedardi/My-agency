## The Challenge
Local supermarkets often struggle with generic POS systems that are slow, complex, and expensive. The client needed a fast, offline-capable billing system that seamlessly integrated with their existing barcode scanners.

## The Solution
I engineered a lightweight desktop and web-based application using a modern tech stack. 

### Key Features Developed:
* **One-Click Invoicing:** Streamlined the checkout process.
* **Real-time Inventory Sync:** Automated low-stock alerts sent directly via WhatsApp.
* **Daily Analytics Dashboard:** Clear visualization of daily sales and top-performing products.

## The Result
Within two weeks of deployment, the client reported a **50% reduction in checkout time** and zero manual errors in inventory counting.
